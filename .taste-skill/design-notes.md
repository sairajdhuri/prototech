# Prototech design decisions

Audience: electronics engineers and purchasing teams assessing a Pune PCB manufacturer. Primary action: submit a board specification for a quotation.

## Inventory

- Established in 1999.
- Narhe manufacturing location in Pune.
- Shed No. 2 at S.R. No. 44/27, Renuka Industrial Estate, Pari Company Road.
- Double-sided and multilayer boards, with published 2–12 layer capability.
- FR4 TG135 glass epoxy laminate.
- All boards electrically tested; Speedy MPP 4504 from MANIA, Germany.
- Pluritec Multistation and Minima CNC equipment from Italy.
- Anger lamination equipment from Germany.
- Orbotech optical inspection equipment from Israel.
- Published 460 × 460 mm finished multilayer board size.
- Six named customers on the original website.
- Original equipment snapshots hosted on the company’s GoDaddy CDN.

## Argument

Hero: this is a PCB manufacturer, with a visual close-up of the object and an immediate quote action.

Highlights: manufacturing history and concrete published specifications establish the scope.

Introduction: prototype and small-volume expertise, supported by the company profile.

Board selector: board construction is the first quote decision, supported by the published product types.

Geometry: actual track, hole and board dimensions make the capability inspectable.

Machinery: named equipment supports the manufacturing story, with original photograph links.

Gallery: visitors can examine board visuals and access original photography.

Customers: only the six names on the existing customer page are used.

Contact: board requirements have a direct path to the sales team.

## Direction and idea

Dealt direction: **The specimen catalogue**, seed **1770032229**.

Idea: On this page, Prototech’s published 2–12 layer range and Pune equipment inventory become an engineering specimen catalogue.

The live feature is the board-construction selector. It uses real product types, updates the URL, and carries the selection into the quote form. Without script, the surrounding capability content and quote links remain available.

Two formal redraws were recorded: the docked-panel hero was replaced with a statement beside a specimen mosaic because the brief requires a large headline and persistent navigation; the warm Young Serif pairing was replaced with the dealt Libre Franklin pairing to suit the requested modern industrial typography. External font downloads were then blocked, so the implemented fallback is a single Arial/Helvetica family. The resulting type is intentionally understated and local.

Palette: the brief’s charcoal, industrial green and warm white. Copper is reserved for keyboard focus. No decorative gradients, glass, card shadows, monospace labels or index numbering.

The original machine image is the selected specimen in the machinery interaction, rather than a decorative disc. The brief requires seven navigation links, so all seven are retained. The closing wordmark is plain enlarged typography; the original logo takes its place in navigation when downloaded.

## Asset constraint

Research through the web tool succeeded. Shell and browser image downloads were blocked by the execution environment. Original image URLs and a reproducible download command remain in the project. Generated PCB artwork and drawn construction diagrams are identified as illustrations. No generated factory, team, customer logo or certification was introduced.

The original products page exposed a gallery title but no extractable image links through the research tool. The download script inspects the actual HTML once internet access is available; until then, the working lightbox contains clearly labelled illustrations and a link to the original gallery.
